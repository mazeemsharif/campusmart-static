About CampusMart

CampusMart was built to solve a simple problem: students need affordable textbooks, calculators, and furniture, and other students have these items collecting dust in their dorms.

We connect buyers and sellers directly on campus, avoiding shipping fees and ensuring you can inspect an item before you buy it.

How it works

  1. Create a free account with your university email.
  2. Post a photo, price, and description of your item.
  3. Meet the buyer safely in a public place on campus.

Safety Tips

  • Always meet in a busy, well-lit public area on campus (like the library lobby).
  • Bring a friend when meeting a buyer or seller for the first time.
  • Test electronics before handing over any money.
